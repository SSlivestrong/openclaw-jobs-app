OpenClaw Jobs SK

A personal, single-user assistant.

OpenClaw Jobs SK is a private tool its owner uses to manage their own job search. It runs on the owner's own server and uses Google APIs (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive) only to access the owner's own Google account: reading recruiter emails, preparing reply drafts and tailored resumes, and scheduling interviews.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
