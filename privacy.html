Privacy Policy: OpenClaw Jobs SK

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who uses this app

OpenClaw Jobs SK is a personal tool used by a single person, its owner, with their own Google account. It is not available to the public.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's Gmail messages and attachments, Google Calendar events and, optionally, Google Drive files, in order to read recruiter emails, prepare reply drafts and tailored resumes, send replies the owner has approved or configured, and schedule interviews.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is processed only on the owner's own server and is used only to provide these features to the owner.
  • Email content may be sent to the AI model providers the owner has configured, solely to process the owner's own requests.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's server.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else, except as needed to provide the features above or to comply with the law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account › Third-party apps & services.

Contact

Questions can be raised through this site's GitHub repository.
